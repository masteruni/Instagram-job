Why your AI emails sound so generic 🤖

It's not the AI, it's the prompt. Give it 3 things and the answer gets way more useful:
1️⃣ Who you are
2️⃣ The details only you know
3️⃣ What you want (tone + length)

Copy this prompt 👇
"I'm a [your role]. [1-2 key details]. Write a short, confident email to [person] asking for [what you want]. Under 80 words."
